Curriculum Essentials

4 Pillars Covered in the Video

Key takeaways conveyed by avatar Anita to empower Indian students and youth online.

1. Credential Security

Anita highlights strong passwords and avoiding repetitive keys across social handles, gaming platforms, and emails.

  • Passphrase method (e.g. 4 random words)
  • Enable 2FA (Two-Factor Authentication)

2. Zero-Trust OTP Defense

No legitimate company, bank, or government officer ever requests OTP over phone, WhatsApp, or SMS.

  • Treat OTP as your personal private ATM PIN
  • Beware of urgency tactics by scammers

3. Verifying Before Forwarding

Youth often fall prey to sensationalized viral posts. Pausing to verify prevents dangerous misinformation spirals.

  • Use reverse image search for viral photos
  • Verify with official portals before sharing

4. Cyber Hygiene & Empathy

Building a respectful digital India where bullying, doxxing, and unsolicited messages are actively rejected.

  • Review privacy settings on Instagram & apps
  • Report suspicious accounts promptly
